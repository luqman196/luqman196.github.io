What we will not do

We don't sell loans you don't need. We take no broker commissions from lenders. Our analysis serves the client who pays for it — no one else. If your case is weak, we'll tell you before a bank does.

Honest limits, stated upfront

Our analysis is reliable, not infallible. Frameworks are tested for Sdn Bhd companies, up to three financial years. Risk flags are indicative indicators — not conclusive findings. We'd rather under-promise.
