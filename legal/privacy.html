Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date on publication]

1. Who we are

Kredit Lab is a brand of Capital Island Sdn Bhd ("we", "us"), a company incorporated in Malaysia with its business address at Lot 5.66, 5th Floor, Wisma Central, Jalan Ampang, 50450 Kuala Lumpur. We can be reached at advisory@kreditlab.my.

2. What we collect

  • Documents you upload — audited financial statements, management accounts, and bank statements, which may contain personal and financial data.
  • Contact details — name, company, phone number, and email address when you purchase a report or submit an enquiry.
  • Payment information — processed by our payment providers; we do not store your card details.

3. How we use it

  • To produce the report or service you purchased.
  • To respond to enquiries you send us.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. What we do NOT do

  • Your documents are not used to train AI models.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not pass your information to lenders or any other party without your explicit instruction.

5. Retention and deletion

Documents you provide for a report are deleted within 7 days of report delivery. Advisory engagement documents are retained for the duration of the engagement and deleted thereafter, except where retention is required by law.

6. Security

Documents are transmitted over encrypted connections and processed on access-controlled systems. No system is perfectly secure; we describe our practices honestly rather than claiming certifications we do not hold.

7. Your rights

Subject to the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by writing to advisory@kreditlab.my. See our PDPA Notice for the bilingual notice required under the Act.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with an updated date.
