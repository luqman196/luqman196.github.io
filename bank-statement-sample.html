FX Classification Methodology

Transactions are classified as FX only when there is clear evidence of foreign currency conversion. Key rules:

  • Default rule: NOT classified as FX unless clear conversion evidence exists
  • TT CREDIT = Telegraphic Transfer (payment method), NOT a currency indicator
  • RENTAS / JANM = Domestic MYR-to-MYR interbank transfers (Real-time Electronic Transfer of Funds and Securities)
  • Voucher codes (GBPV, USDP) in reference fields = internal bank numbering, not currency denominations
  • True FX requires: conversion rate, foreign currency amount, SWIFT codes, or foreign beneficiary with non-MYR amounts
