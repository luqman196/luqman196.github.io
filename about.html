Kredit Lab exists because bank rejections come with no explanation — and the people best placed to explain them almost never leave the bank.

Why We Exist

Somebody reads your file before you ever speak to them.

By the time a Malaysian SME hears an answer from a bank, the real work is long finished. Somewhere, someone has already spread the accounts, run the ratios, read the bank statements line by line, and formed a view. The business owner is almost never shown that view — not the reasoning, not the numbers, often not even a reason.

Between us we spent a long time being those people. We know exactly what gets looked at, in what order, and what quietly sinks an application before anyone picks up the phone. Kredit Lab is that reading, done for you instead of about you.

Every analysis we produce is graded against frameworks derived from the real credit parameters used in the banking world. Not theory. Not textbook ratios. The lenses a credit officer actually applies before your file reaches a committee — encoded into tooling we built ourselves, then read and signed off by a person before it reaches you.

The Lab

What we're building next.

There is a reason the word "Lab" is in the name. New capability goes into the analysis a few times a quarter. Rather than tell you we are "constantly innovating" — which every company says and none of them date — here is the actual log.

  • LiveAug 2026

    Cash flow analysis

    The Financial Health Report now derives a full cash flow statement from your audited accounts — not just profit on paper, but where the money actually came from and where it went.

  • LiveSep 2026

    Internal credit grade

    A rating on the Financial Health Report, graded against a defined scorecard with the working shown — so it is a measurement you can argue with, not an opinion you have to accept.

  • LiveSep 2026

    Statement coverage checks

    Before any bank statement analysis is trusted, the system verifies that every month and every page is actually present. A gap gets flagged, not quietly averaged over — because a missing month is exactly where the awkward transactions tend to live.

  • Livethrough 2026

    More bank formats, read directly

    Malaysian bank statement layouts differ wildly, and converted-to-Excel statements lose things. We add banks one at a time, each tested to the sen against the bank's own declared totals before it goes anywhere near a client report.

  • In testingSep 2026

    Management accounts builder

    Monthly management accounts reconstructed from bank statements, for the very common case where your last audited accounts are already eighteen months stale. Being tested against real cases now.

  • In development—

    Financial projections

    Forward cash flow and repayment capacity — the question a credit officer asks last and hardest. Under construction; it will ship when it can be trusted, not when it would be convenient to announce.

Nothing on this list reaches your report before it has been tested against real cases. If a line says in testing or in development, it is not in your report, and we will not imply otherwise.

Track Record

Ours is private. Deliberately.

We don't publish deal tables or "RM-something million arranged" banners. Those numbers belong to clients who trusted us with their accounts, and dressing them up as marketing is not something we are willing to do — anonymised or not.

When we meet, we will walk you through comparable cases properly: same industry, same facility type, what worked, what didn't. Until then, judge the work rather than the tally. The sample reports are real ones with the names taken out.

Our Philosophy

What we will not do.

No selling you loans

We don't push financing you don't need. If the honest answer is "fix these three things first and apply in six months," that's the answer you'll get.

No broker commissions

We take no commissions from lenders. Our analysis serves the client who pays for it — which means it can afford to be honest.

No false certainty

Our analysis is reliable, not infallible. Flags are indicative, not conclusive. Nobody can guarantee an approval — and we never will.
