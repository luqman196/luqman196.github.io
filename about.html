The Lab

We're not finished, and we don't pretend to be.

There is a reason the word "Lab" is in the name. We run our own research and development, and the analysis behind your report is not a fixed product — every real case teaches us something, and what it teaches goes back into the engines.

Cash flow analysis and an internal credit grade both came out of that work. Management accounts rebuilt from bank statements, and forward financial projections, are what we are building next.

Nothing reaches a client report until it has been tested against real cases. When something new is ready you will see it in the report itself — not in an announcement.

Track Record

Ours is private. Deliberately.

We don't publish deal tables or "RM-something million arranged" banners. Those numbers belong to clients who trusted us with their accounts, and dressing them up as marketing is not something we are willing to do — anonymised or not.

When we meet, we will walk you through comparable cases properly: same industry, same facility type, what worked, what didn't. Until then, judge the work rather than the tally. The sample reports are real ones with the names taken out.

Our Philosophy

What we will not do.

No selling you loans

We don't push financing you don't need. If the honest answer is "fix these three things first and apply in six months," that's the answer you'll get.

No broker commissions

We take no commissions from lenders. Our analysis serves the client who pays for it — which means it can afford to be honest.

No false certainty

Our analysis is reliable, not infallible. Flags are indicative, not conclusive. Nobody can guarantee an approval — and we never will.
