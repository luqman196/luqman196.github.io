The Systems

Two engines. One judgement layer.

Bank Statement Analyzer (BSA)

Bank statement PDFs in — structured cashflow intelligence out.

• Transaction classification and counterparty analysis
• Cashflow forensics: real revenue vs. round-tripping patterns
• Risk flags — indicative indicators for reviewer attention
• Month-completeness and statement-integrity checks
• Structured HTML and Excel outputs

Financial Statement Analyzer (FSA)

Audited and management accounts in — structured analysis out.

• Full financial spreading across up to three financial years
• Ratio and cashflow analysis with consistency validation
• A Bank-Readiness Rating summarising the credit posture
• Written analysis a credit officer can lift into a paper
• Structured HTML and Excel outputs

Analysis outputs are decision support, not decisions. Risk flags are indicative indicators for human review — not conclusive findings.
