End to End

What an engagement covers.

  1. 1

    Financial assessment

    We analyse your accounts and bank statements the way a credit officer will — and tell you honestly whether you're ready to apply, or what to fix first.

  2. 2

    Structuring

    The right facility, the right amount, the right tenure. Most rejections we see were the wrong ask, not the wrong business.

  3. 3

    Bank selection

    Different lenders have different appetites. We match your profile to the institutions most likely to say yes — before you spend months finding out the hard way.

  4. 4

    Submission preparation

    A complete, well-presented application that answers the credit team's questions before they're asked.

  5. 5

    Negotiation support

    When offers come back, we help you understand the terms and negotiate what's negotiable.
