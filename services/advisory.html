Track Record

We don't publish deal tables.

Every case we have worked on belongs to a client who handed us their audited accounts and their bank statements. Those numbers are not ours to advertise — not even anonymised, not even rounded, not even as a total.

So we won't be telling you we have arranged RM-something million across such-and-such industries. Everyone says that, almost nobody can be checked on it, and it tells you nothing about whether we can read your file.

What we will do: when we meet, we will walk you through comparable cases — same industry, same facility type, what worked, what didn't, and where it nearly fell over. In the room, properly, with context. That is worth more than a number on a website.

Until then, judge the work instead. The sample reports are genuine client reports with the names removed — exactly what you would receive.
