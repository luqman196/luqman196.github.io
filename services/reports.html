FAQ

Common questions.

Who prepares the report?

Analysis frameworks built by a former banker, executed by our systems and reviewed under our quality checks. A real person stands behind every report — see who we are.

What documents do I need?

Financial Health Report: your audited financial statements (up to 3 years). Bank Statement Report: your business bank statements. PDF copies are fine.

How fast is delivery?

Within 1 working day after we receive complete, legible documents.

Can I get a refund?

Because each report is bespoke analysis work, fees aren't refundable once processing begins — but if we can't process your documents, we'll tell you upfront and refund in full, and we correct any fundamental factual error at no charge. Full detail in the terms.

Will this guarantee my loan gets approved?

No — and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. What it does is remove the guesswork: you'll know how a bank reads your file before you commit to an application.
