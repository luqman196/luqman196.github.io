FAQ

Common questions.

Who prepares the report?

Our consultants, working with analysis systems we built in-house. A person reviews every report before it reaches you — see who we are.

What documents do I need?

Financial Health Report: audited financial statements, up to 3 years. Bank Statement Report: business bank statements. PDF copies are fine — we'll confirm the exact list when you message us.

How much, and how fast?

RM500 per report, delivered within 1 working day of complete documents.

Can I get a refund?

Fees aren't refundable once analysis begins — but if we can't work with your documents, we say so upfront and refund in full, and we correct any factual error at no charge. Details in the terms.

Will this guarantee my loan gets approved?

No — and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. It removes the guesswork: you'll know how a bank reads your file before you apply.
