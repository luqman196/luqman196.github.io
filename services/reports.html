After Delivery

The report is not the end of it.

Handing an SME owner a credit analysis and walking away is how people end up more confused than before they started. So we don't do that.

What the session covers

15 minutes by phone or online meeting, with the consultant who reviewed your file:

· What the report says, in plain language — no jargon left standing
· Which findings a bank will care about, and which are noise
· What is fixable, what takes time, and what isn't worth chasing
· Whether you are ready to apply, or should wait

What it is not

Being straight with you about the boundary, so nobody feels short-changed:

· It is a walkthrough of your report — not a full advisory engagement
· We don't prepare or submit your application in this session
· If your case needs hands-on work, we'll say so and explain what advisory would involve — you're free to say no
· No commission, no lender introductions attached to it

FAQ

Common questions.

Who prepares the report?

Our consultants, working with analysis systems we built in-house. A person reviews every report before it reaches you — see who we are.

What documents do I need?

Financial Health Report: audited financial statements, up to 3 years. Bank Statement Report: business bank statements. PDF copies are fine — we'll confirm the exact list when you message us.

How much, and how fast?

RM500 per report, typically delivered within 1 working day of complete documents. If your case needs more time, we say so upfront.

Do I have to figure the report out on my own?

No. A walkthrough with the consultant who reviewed your file is included in the RM500: a 15-minute phone call or online meeting after delivery to go through the findings and answer your questions on the report. It is a walkthrough of the report, not a full advisory engagement; if your case needs hands-on work, we'll tell you what that would involve rather than leave you guessing.

Can I get a refund?

Fees aren't refundable once analysis begins — but if we can't work with your documents, we say so upfront and refund in full, and we correct any factual error at no charge. Details in the terms.

Will this guarantee my loan gets approved?

No — and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. It removes the guesswork: you'll know how a bank reads your file before you apply.
