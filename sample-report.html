The cash conversion cycle is negative in all three periods (-126.94 days in FY2023, -100.67 days in FY2024 and -279.53 days in FY2025), indicating the business is funded by its payables rather than needing to fund a working-capital gap. This is a liquidity/solvency concern rather than a working-capital-financing need, and is addressed under areas of concern.

📊 Key Observations

Revenue Trend

Revenue declined from RM45.90m (FY2023) to RM39.29m (FY2024), a 14.4% contraction, before surging to RM158.15m in the FY2025 management account — a roughly four-fold increase. In FY2023-24 the revenue was heavily concentrated in a single customer, Vestigo (RM31.43m and RM33.29m respectively, ~70-85% of turnover), while the FY2025 figure is unitemised in the draft MA and appears to reflect the ramp-up of several offshore projects (MOPU Banang, TFSO Sepat, Jitang Ext). The FY2025 scale-up is significant but unaudited and should be corroborated.

Profitability Trend

The company swung from a restated net loss of RM2.86m in FY2023 (dragged down by restated finance costs of RM6.03m) to net profit of RM1.35m in FY2024, then to RM10.00m in the FY2025 MA. Gross margin is high but declining (67.5% FY2023, 76.7% FY2024, 41.9% FY2025), the FY2025 fall reflecting higher direct project costs of RM91.92m. EBITDA is consistently strong (RM27.57m, RM27.12m, RM53.74m) as depreciation is a very large non-cash charge.

Liquidity Position

Liquidity is weak and deteriorating. The current ratio was 0.35x (FY2023), recovered to 1.04x (FY2024), then fell to just 0.26x in FY2025 as current liabilities ballooned to RM157.09m against current assets of RM40.85m. The FY2025 cash ratio of 0.04x (RM6.97m unrestricted cash vs RM157.09m current liabilities) is critically thin and points to material short-term refinancing risk.

Working Capital Cycle

The cash conversion cycle is negative throughout (-126.94, -100.67 and -279.53 days), meaning suppliers/creditors fund the operating cycle. However this is driven by heavily stretched payables (creditor days rising to 330 in FY2025 on RM83.04m trade creditors) rather than efficient terms. OWC is -RM1.26m (FY2023), +RM4.56m (FY2024) and -RM61.29m (FY2025); no dedicated working-capital facility is structurally required but the creditor stretch is a solvency signal.

Debt Structure

Total borrowings rose from RM47.81m (FY2023) to RM33.56m (FY2024) then to RM91.78m in FY2025, lifting gearing from 0.87x to 0.59x and up to 1.30x. The FY2025 profile is skewed to short-term debt — RM69.30m of bank loan plus RM0.43m overdraft sit in current liabilities against only RM22.04m of non-current term loans. Borrowings are secured by the freehold building, the FPSO/FSO vessel and joint-and-several director guarantees.

Funding Position

Funding structure moved from matched in FY2024 (-0.66% gap) to a severe mismatch in FY2025, where RM213.68m of non-current assets are funded only RM97.45m long-term, leaving a RM116.23m gap (54.4% of NCA) covered by short-term liabilities. Capitalising the RM50.04m FSO Sepat asset without matched long-term funding is the principal cause and makes the balance sheet fragile.

Asset Base

The asset base is dominated by vessel and project equipment. Net PPE fell from RM115.63m (FY2023) to RM91.94m (FY2024) as depreciation of RM23.77m outpaced additions, then jumped to RM213.57m in FY2025 with the RM50.04m FSO Sepat plus RM163.53m of other fixed assets recognised. The freehold building (RM6.5m carrying) and the vessel are pledged as facility security.

Related Party Exposure

Related-party exposure is modest in the audited years: amounts due from related parties/subsidiaries totalled RM0.38m (FY2023) and RM0.09m (FY2024), and amounts due to related companies were RM0.41m (FY2023, nil FY2024). The FY2025 MA does not separately disclose related-party balances, so this cannot be re-verified for the latest period.

Dividend Policy

No dividends were declared or paid in any period (the directors did not recommend a dividend for FY2024). All earnings are retained, consistent with the company's need to fund its heavy asset base and reduce short-term borrowings.
