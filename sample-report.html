📊 Key Observations

Revenue Trend

Revenue grew strongly and consistently from RM16.84 million (FY2023) to RM21.38 million (FY2024, +26.9%) and RM26.92 million (FY2025, +25.9%). Growth is broad-based across both streams, with fabrication contracts rising from RM11.43 million to RM18.96 million and maintenance and repair services from RM5.41 million to RM7.96 million over the three years.

Profitability Trend

Net profit after tax rose each year from RM832,715 to RM1,043,210 to RM1,345,470, and net margin held steady around 4.9%-5.0%. However, gross profit margin compressed each year from 22.03% to 20.49% to 18.97%, as materials, subcontract charges and machinery upkeep grew faster than revenue — a trend to watch even though absolute gross profit rose to RM5.11 million.

Liquidity Position

The current ratio is healthy throughout at 1.95x, 1.75x and 1.77x, comfortably above the 1.25x benchmark, and the quick ratio remains sound at 1.44x in FY2025. However, the cash ratio collapsed from 0.50x to just 0.05x as free cash was consumed by capex and receivables, with the company opening a bank overdraft (RM612,480 drawn) in FY2025.

Working Capital Cycle

The cash conversion cycle (primary indicator) is positive and lengthening markedly — 28.31 days (FY2023), 36.43 days (FY2024) and 53.12 days (FY2025) — driven by debtor days extending to 73.36 days and creditor days shortening to 45.01 days. Operating working capital (supporting indicator) rose in step to RM4.20 million, confirming a genuine and growing need for revolving working capital financing.

Debt Structure

Total borrowings rose from RM1.45 million to RM3.39 million (following the RM2.4 million Term Loan II factory drawdown in FY2024) then to RM3.78 million in FY2025 as an overdraft appeared. Gearing peaked at 0.71x in FY2024 and eased to 0.62x in FY2025, well within prudent limits, and liabilities-to-equity of 1.20x is comfortably below the 4.0x benchmark. Debt is predominantly term-structured against long-lived assets, with hire purchase (RM480,000) financing vehicles and machinery.

Funding Position

The funding structure is sound and fully matched: non-current assets of RM5.37 million are entirely covered by long-term funding of RM8.89 million (equity RM6.12 million plus non-current liabilities RM2.77 million) in FY2025, leaving a favourable funding gap of RM3.52 million (-65.6% of NCA). Long-term assets are not being financed by short-term liabilities in any period.

Asset Base

Total assets expanded from RM8.41 million to RM13.48 million, led by the FY2024 factory investment that lifted PPE from RM2.32 million to RM5.04 million and then RM5.37 million. Fixed deposits of RM440,980 are pledged to Bank A Berhad and are therefore restricted; the balance sheet is otherwise unencumbered by intangibles.

Related Party Exposure

The only related-party exposure is an amount due from a director — RM350,000 at FY2024, reduced to RM180,000 at FY2025 — which is unsecured, interest-free and repayable on demand. The reduction is credit-positive, but the balance remains a non-operating use of company funds that is deducted in arriving at adjusted tangible net worth of RM5.94 million.

Dividend Policy

Dividend policy is conservative. A single interim dividend of RM400,000 (in respect of FY2023) was paid during FY2024; no dividends were paid in FY2023 or FY2025. Earnings are overwhelmingly retained, driving retained earnings from RM3.13 million to RM5.12 million and supporting the self-funded balance-sheet growth.

✅Positive Trends
  • Strong, sustained revenue growth: Revenue rose 26.9% then 25.9% to reach RM26.92 million in FY2025, with both fabrication (RM18.96 million) and maintenance (RM7.96 million) streams growing. This demonstrates a stable, expanding order book and a diversified two-line revenue mix.
  • Consistent and rising profitability: Net profit after tax increased every year to RM1,345,470 in FY2025 with net margin holding near 5.0%. Return on equity is strong and stable at 20%-22%, indicating efficient use of shareholder capital.
  • Robust EBITDA debt-service coverage: EBITDA DSCR is well above the 1.25x benchmark throughout (3.29x, 2.93x, 3.39x), supported by EBITDA of RM2.48 million against total debt service of RM731,350 in FY2025. Interest coverage of 13.52x provides a wide cushion on finance costs.
  • Healthy liquidity and low leverage: The current ratio of 1.77x and quick ratio of 1.44x in FY2025 sit comfortably above benchmark, while liabilities-to-equity of 1.20x and gearing of 0.62x are conservative. This gives meaningful headroom to raise additional facilities.
  • Matched, self-sustaining funding structure: Non-current assets of RM5.37 million are fully covered by RM8.89 million of long-term funding, giving a favourable funding gap of RM3.52 million. The factory (RM2.65 million) was correctly financed with a 15-year term loan rather than short-term debt.
  • Clean audit history and reducing related-party exposure: Both FY2024 and FY2025 carry unqualified audit opinions with no emphasis of matter or going-concern issues. The amount due from a director was actively reduced from RM350,000 to RM180,000 in FY2025.
⚠️Areas Requiring Attention
  • Weak cash conversion and sub-1.0x cash DSCR: Despite strong EBITDA coverage, operating cash flow of only RM454,380 in FY2025 produced a cash DSCR of 0.62x (and 0.49x in FY2024), both below 1.0x. Debt service was in practice met by drawing the overdraft and running down cash rather than from operations, which is the single most important credit signal in this file. HIGH
  • Lengthening working capital cycle and rising receivables: The cash conversion cycle nearly doubled from 28.31 days to 53.12 days over three years as debtor days climbed to 73.36 days and creditor days fell to 45.01 days. Receivables absorbed RM1.55 million of cash in FY2025 and the working capital requirement now approaches RM3.9 million. HIGH
  • Undersized revolving facility versus working capital need: The only revolving line is a RM1,000,000 overdraft, drawn RM612,480 at FY2025, against a working capital requirement of roughly RM3.9 million. This structural shortfall forced cash depletion and is unsustainable if receivables continue to grow. MEDIUM
  • Gross margin erosion: Gross profit margin fell every year from 22.03% to 18.97% as materials, subcontract and machinery-upkeep costs outpaced revenue. Continued compression would materially reduce the EBITDA cushion that currently supports debt service. MEDIUM
  • Very low cash buffer and emergence of overdraft reliance: The cash ratio fell to 0.05x and the company moved to a net overdrawn cash position (closing cash of negative RM378,175 including the RM612,480 overdraft) in FY2025. Free cash flow was negative in both FY2024 (-RM2.85 million) and FY2025 (-RM155,620). MEDIUM
🎯Strategic Recommendations
  1. [PHIGH] Working capital financing: Restructure and increase the revolving working capital facility from the current RM1,000,000 overdraft toward the ~RM3.9 million working capital requirement, ideally via invoice/trade financing tied to the receivables book, to stop debt service being funded from cash depletion.
  2. [PHIGH] Receivables management: Tighten collections to reverse the rise in debtor days from 64.58 to 73.36 days; each 10-day reduction would release roughly RM0.7 million of cash given FY2025 revenue of RM26.92 million.
  3. [PMEDIUM] Margin protection: Investigate the gross margin decline from 22.03% to 18.97%, focusing on materials pricing and subcontract/machinery-upkeep cost control, and reflect cost inflation in contract pricing.
  4. [PMEDIUM] Related party balance: Continue reducing the amount due from a director (RM180,000 at FY2025) and formalise repayment terms; monitor for any re-advances that would weaken tangible net worth.
  5. [PLOW] Liquidity monitoring: Rebuild an unrestricted cash buffer and monitor cash DSCR alongside EBITDA DSCR each period, given the current divergence between the two measures.
